# Actionable insights

Code: `ml/prism_ml/insights/engine.py` (sentences) and `references.py` (sources). The insight component
**does not classify**. It explains the status it receives (from the trained model or an operational
state) using the actual measurements, data-quality findings and referenced guidance. The sentences are
fixed, reviewed text chosen by rules; they are not generated by a trained model.

## Status meanings

| Status | Source | Meaning for the farmer |
|---|---|---|
| Good | trained model | Readings resemble expert-labelled favourable conditions. Keep routine monitoring. |
| Needs Attention | trained model | Readings resemble early-concern conditions. Check airflow, water and the pigs. |
| High Risk | trained model | Readings resemble marked-concern conditions. Assess the pigs now and cool them. |
| Uncertain | operational | The model can't give a reliable answer; treat as unknown, **not** safe. |
| Classification Unavailable | operational | No validated model (current state) or a model error. Guidance only. |
| Insufficient Data | operational | Readings missing or stale. |
| Sensor Check Required | operational | Device fault, out-of-range value, flat frame, clock or calibration problem. |

## Sentence logic

Every response contains a status sentence and, when readings exist, an observation sentence ("Latest
readings: air temperature …, humidity …, warmest thermal-camera reading …") with the caveat that the
camera measures apparent surface temperature (±2.5 °C), not body temperature.

* **Good:** status → observations → measurements resembling Good training sessions (from the model's
  per-class medians) → continue monitoring.
* **Needs Attention:** status → observations → supporting measurements → why it matters (less activity,
  less feed, faster breathing) → improve airflow, check drinkers (clean, cool, good flow) → check
  animal signs → consider misting where safe (the farmer decides; PRISM never switches the sprinkler on
  from an alert).
* **High Risk:** status ("Assess the pigs now") → observations → supporting measurements → why
  (body temperature can keep rising) → maximise ventilation and air movement, water access, misting or
  wetting the pigs where safe (keep water away from electrical equipment) → avoid moving, handling or
  treating pigs in the hottest hours → **contact a veterinarian** for open-mouth breathing, heavy
  panting, collapse, or a pig not rising.
* **Thermal statuses** also show "This status comes from PRISM's trained model (model confidence X %) …
  not a diagnosis."
* **Classification Unavailable / Uncertain:** observations; THI context *only* when THI > 84
  ("reference information, not a PRISM classification"); the animal-sign checklist; water and
  ventilation basics.
* **Insufficient Data / Sensor Check Required:** what is wrong (up to four data-quality messages), check
  power/Wi-Fi/wiring/lens, observe the pigs in person.
* **Water:** tank < 25 % → refill sentence; water sensor invalid → check the tank by hand.

## Safety rules for the wording

* Never say a pig is healthy, sick, or has heat stroke; no medication or dosing advice (enforced by a
  test that scans all sentences).
* Operational states never use reassuring words ("favourable") and say "treat as unknown, not safe".
* Water-based cooling is always qualified with "where it is safe to do so".
* The animals override the sensors: every non-Good response tells the farmer to look at the pigs.

## When to contact a veterinarian

Any pig with open-mouth breathing or heavy panting that does not ease after cooling, collapse,
inability or unwillingness to rise, or any sign of illness, whatever PRISM displays.

## Limitations

English only. A Filipino/Tagalog translation should be reviewed with farmers and an expert before use.
The recommendations come from a European welfare review and still need review against local
Philippine smallholder practice (housing, water supply, and the absence of mechanical ventilation in many pens).
